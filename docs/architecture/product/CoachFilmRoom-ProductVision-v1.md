# Coach Film Room — Product Vision v1

Status: Certified v1

Classification: Foundational Product Architecture

Purpose:

Describe the ideal Coach Film Room experience as a product vision. This document defines what Film Room must be. It does not define interfaces, components, or implementation.

---

## 1. Film Room as a Learning Environment

Coach Film Room is not a video player with notes attached.
It is a learning environment where athletic evidence becomes teachable.

The purpose of Film Room is athletic improvement through guided review.

A successful Film Room session leaves the athlete with:

- a clearer understanding of what happened
- a named priority for what changes next
- confidence that the coach's judgment is the guide

If review does not produce learning, Film Room has failed — regardless of playback quality.

---

## Relationship to Coaching Intelligence

Coach Film Room is one learning environment within the broader MatMind Coaching Intelligence platform. Film Room expresses the principles established by the Coaching Intelligence Charter during guided review. Future Coaching Intelligence capabilities may exist outside Film Room while remaining governed by the same constitutional principles.

---

## 2. Video Is the Hero

Video is the primary artifact of Film Room.

All coaching meaning, commentary, and guidance orbit the video.
Nothing in Film Room may compete with the primacy of the footage.

Supporting materials exist to illuminate the video.
They must never displace it.

The athlete should always know: we are watching this moment because it matters.

---

## 3. Coaching Comes First

Film Room exists to deliver coaching, not to display media.

The coach's interpretation defines:

- what deserves attention
- what can be ignored
- what the athlete should take away

Playback serves coaching intent.
Coaching intent does not serve playback novelty.

When coaching meaning and media convenience conflict, coaching meaning wins.

---

## 4. Learning Before Entertainment

Film Room is instructional, not recreational.

Engagement that does not produce understanding is not success.
Smooth consumption without reflection is not success.

Design and experience choices must favor:

- clarity over spectacle
- focus over abundance
- retention over novelty

Entertainment patterns that dilute learning do not belong in Film Room.

---

## 5. Progressive Disclosure

Not everything should be visible at once.

Film Room reveals depth as learning readiness increases:

1. First: the moment that matters
2. Then: the coach's framing of that moment
3. Then: supporting context and related evidence
4. Only when needed: deeper history and adjacent patterns

Overwhelm is a product defect.
Progressive disclosure protects attention so coaching can land.

---

## 6. Reflection Over Consumption

The highest-value Film Room outcome is reflection that changes practice.

Consumption is watching.
Reflection is understanding that alters behavior.

Film Room must create space for:

- pause
- re-watching with intent
- coach-directed emphasis
- athlete acknowledgment of the lesson

A session that is finished but not internalized is incomplete.

---

## 7. The Ideal Experience

In the ideal Film Room experience:

- The athlete enters with a clear coaching purpose.
- Video occupies the center of attention.
- The coach's commentary guides what to notice.
- Complexity appears only when it serves the lesson.
- The session ends with a named takeaway and a path into focused practice.

Film Room closes the gap between "what happened" and "what we will work on."

---

## 8. Non-Goals

Film Room is not:

- a general-purpose media library
- a social highlight reel
- a statistics dashboard with video attached
- an entertainment feed
- a replacement for live coaching

Those forms may share media. They do not share Film Room's purpose.

---

## Vision Boundary

This document defines product vision only.

It does not authorize:

- UI layout
- component architecture
- playback technology choices
- implementation sequencing

All Film Room product work must remain consistent with this vision.
