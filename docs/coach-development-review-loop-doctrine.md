# MatMind — Coach Development Review Loop Doctrine

## Core Principle

The coach workspace is not:

* a CRM
* a roster manager
* a weekly checklist
* a stats dashboard
* a publishing tool

The coach workspace is:

# an athlete understanding system.

The purpose of the coach workspace is to help a coach answer:

* Where is this athlete developmentally?
* What patterns are emerging?
* Is coaching transferring into execution?
* What keeps recurring?
* What matters most right now?
* What reinforcement is needed next?
* Is the athlete evolving?

The coach should leave the workspace feeling:

# “I understand this athlete more clearly.”

Not:

# “I updated records.”

---

# The Canonical Coach Cognition Loop

The coach review experience should follow this exact psychological sequence:

## 1. Athlete State

### “Where is this athlete right now?”

The workspace should immediately orient the coach around:

* current developmental state
* confidence level
* execution quality
* reinforcement stage
* consistency
* behavioral tendencies
* pressure response
* developmental momentum

This is:

# interpreted athlete state.

Not metrics.

Examples:

* Applying naturally in training
* Strong technically but hesitant live
* Reverting under pressure
* Building consistency
* Confidence improving week-over-week
* Executing first layer but stalling on transitions

This layer becomes the emotional anchor of the workspace.

---

## 2. Longitudinal Guidance

### “What matters most over time?”

This is the standing developmental direction.

Not:

* a weekly task
* a temporary note
* a checklist item

This is:

# coach developmental interpretation.

It persists across weeks until intentionally changed.

It represents:

* recurring developmental emphasis
* strategic identity shaping
* recurring gap reinforcement
* long-term coaching belief

Examples:

* Needs stronger first attack commitment
* Build confidence under pace
* Simplify passing decision tree
* Reinforce positional scoring awareness
* Develop pressure tolerance before expansion

This is the highest-authority coach-authored layer in the system.

---

## 3. Reinforcement Cycle

### “What are we actively reinforcing right now?”

This is the operational weekly layer.

It exists to support the longitudinal guidance.

This includes:

* current weekly focus
* what we sharpened with coach
* mission links
* study resources
* parent reinforcement payloads

This layer is:

# temporary and operational.

Not longitudinal memory.

This is where:

* weekly publishing
* parent reinforcement
* family huddles
* current reinforcement cycles

belong.

---

## 4. Observation Memory

### “Is coaching showing up in behavior?”

This is the developmental intelligence layer.

The purpose is not:

* journaling
* dumping notes
* session recaps

The purpose is:

# recurring interpretation accumulation.

Observations should help coaches understand:

* transfer
* execution consistency
* confidence shifts
* recurring breakdowns
* emotional tendencies
* strategic adaptation
* pressure behavior
* reinforcement response

Examples:

* Executes well until pace increases
* Strong response after verbal reminder
* Hesitates entering first attack
* Confidence rising after successful rounds
* Returning to old habits under fatigue
* Better recovery after mistakes
* Increasing positional patience

This becomes:

# longitudinal coaching memory.

Over time, this layer powers:

* developmental timelines
* recurring pattern detection
* premium athlete intelligence
* AI-supported synthesis
* voice-note interpretation memory
* strategic evolution tracking

This is one of the deepest long-term moats in MatMind.

---

## 5. Parent Reinforcement

### “What should the family understand and reinforce?”

Parent surfaces are downstream reinforcement systems.

The family should receive:

* simplified direction
* encouragement framing
* reinforcement understanding
* developmental visibility
* emotional clarity

Parents should NOT receive:

* raw coach cognition
* private interpretation
* unresolved concerns
* tactical coaching memory
* longitudinal internal observations

The parent experience should feel:

# supportive and developmental.

Not analytical.

# Authority Hierarchy

## Highest Authority

### Coach-authored interpretation

* longitudinal guidance
* recurring observations
* developmental framing
* strategic identity direction
* reinforcement priorities

This is canonical human coaching intelligence.

---

## Medium Authority

### Athlete proof systems

* training logs
* competition outcomes
* execution evidence
* training proof
* match breakdowns

Proof supports interpretation.

Proof does not replace interpretation.

---

## Lowest Authority

### AI support systems

* encouragement framing
* parent-friendly translation
* synthesis suggestions
* reflection prompts
* language softening
* summarization

AI assists clarity.

AI does not define athlete truth.

The interface must visually preserve this hierarchy.
