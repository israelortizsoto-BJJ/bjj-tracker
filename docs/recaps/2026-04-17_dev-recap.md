---

## Root cause
`getCoachSyncApiBaseUrl()` / `isCoachSyncConfigured()` (`src/config/coachSync.ts`) require **`EXPO_PUBLIC_COACH_SYNC_BASE_URL`** or **`extra.coachSyncBaseUrl`** in the **built** app. Without them, Join shows **Coach sync URL missing** and does not call the worker.

## Critical lesson
When debugging, validate in this order:

1. **Environment**
2. **Runtime** (which app binary is actually running)
3. **Then code**

Today’s issue would have been misdiagnosed as a sync bug without checking environment first.
